Conditions We Support

Care for pain, recovery and mobility

🦴

Joint & Back Pain

Neck pain, low back pain, knee osteoarthritis, shoulder stiffness and postural strain.

🩹

Post-Surgical Rehab

Structured recovery programmes after orthopaedic surgery, fractures and immobilisation.

🧠

Neuro Rehabilitation

Supportive therapy for stroke recovery, balance training and weakness management.

⚡

Sports & Soft Tissue

Sprains, muscle strains, tendon problems and return-to-activity conditioning.

📍

Acupuncture

Traditional needle-based therapy for selected pain conditions, by trained practitioners.

👴

Elderly Mobility

Fall-prevention exercises, gait training and strength maintenance for seniors.

How It Works

Plan-based sessions, measured progress

  • Assessment of condition, pain levels and functional goals
  • Individualised session plan with realistic milestones
  • Home exercise programme so progress continues between visits
  • Periodic re-evaluation with referrals when needed
🕐 Afternoon sessions, by appointment. Slots are limited — booking ahead ensures your preferred time.
