FAQ

Common questions

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?
Evening Allopathy OP (5:30–9 PM) accepts walk-ins, but booking ahead reduces waiting. AYUSH morning consultations, physiotherapy, acupuncture, naturopathy and doorstep visits are by appointment.
How do I book an appointment?
Use the online booking page — your details reach our team directly on WhatsApp. You can also call +91 99402 79752.
Which system of medicine should I choose?
You don't have to decide alone. Our professionals assess your condition and explain the appropriate options, including when another system or a specialist referral is more suitable. Choose "Not Sure — Please Guide Me" while booking.
Is there a pharmacy and lab at the clinic?
Yes — a prescription-based medical dispensary operates inside the clinic, and Thyrocare laboratory investigations can be arranged at competitive prices.
Do you provide care at home?
Doorstep consultation is available based on availability and prior arrangement for those who cannot travel. It is not a substitute for emergency services.
